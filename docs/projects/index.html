Personal projects

Each labelled by its real stage. None is a launched business.

  • ORBIT · application command center

    My own tool for running a job search as a pipeline: discovery, fit check against my verified profile, tailored documents, proof of submission and follow-up. Modules: Mission Control, Job Radar, Profile Core, Launchpad, Flightpath, Signals.

    In daily use. Designed, directed and tested by me.

  • KoraJobs · automated job pipeline

    Pulls offers from the France Travail API, scores fit with a language model against three career lanes, and prepares tailored applications for review.

    Prototype. Prototype for personal use.

  • Teranga · marketplace for The Gambia

    Online marketplace for The Gambia and its diaspora: categories, diaspora gifting, payment plans and an AI shopping host, Dalal, designed to speak English, Wolof, Mandinka and Pulaar.

    Prototype. Working prototype. Not launched; no sales.

  • Kafo · digital trust layer (concept)

    Concept for Gambian public services: identity, licences, vehicle records and ownership transfer, with officer verification and assisted service.

    Concept. Pre-production concept. No government partnership.
