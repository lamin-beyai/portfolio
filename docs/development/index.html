How a development consultancy earns its money, and where I fitted in

SMD is a Gambian consultancy: governments, UN agencies and donors hire it for training, technical assistance, research and evaluations. Revenue comes assignment by assignment, won through proposals and paid against approved deliverables. In three years I worked across almost every step of that cycle, through four roles.

Four roles, one progression

  1. Aug 2021 – Mar 2022

    Research & Programme Intern

    • Baseline studies and field data collection.
    • Desk research and evidence synthesis.
    • Proposal writing support and early drafts of programme reports.

  2. Apr 2022 – Nov 2022

    Admin & Finance Officer

    • Budgets, expense processing, invoicing and reconciliations.
    • Payment monitoring and settlement, procurement coordination, record keeping.
    • Financial reporting; started the redevelopment of the firm's website.

  3. Nov 2022 – Dec 2023

    Learning & Development Officer

    • Designed and delivered training on leadership, governance and data literacy.
    • Wrote training materials and facilitation guides.
    • Contributed to proposals that won contracts with UNDP, UNESCO, WFP and credit-union associations.

  4. Dec 2023 – Sep 2024

    Program Manager, Projects & M&E

    • Climate, youth and governance assignments from planning to evaluation: workplans, budgets, status and risk tracking.
    • Worked on indicators, logframes and survey tools; coordinated field survey teams.
    • Automated survey processing and indicator tracking in Python; presented results to funders in Power BI.

The types of work

Baseline
Measures the starting value of a programme's indicators so later change can be attributed. Instruments, sampling and field quality decide whether the endline will mean anything.
Evaluation
Judges whether a programme reached its outcomes, usually on the OECD-DAC criteria: relevance, coherence, effectiveness, efficiency, impact and sustainability.
Risk analysis
Maps hazards, exposure and vulnerability, here with a gender lens, so adaptation is targeted where it matters.
Strategy
Turns findings into a roadmap: what continues after funding ends, who owns it, how it is measured.
Assessment
A focused study of a population or system, such as skills supply and demand, to inform programme design.
Institutional support
Work inside a client institution on its plans, processes and performance metrics.
