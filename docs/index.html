The work behind it

Capital Fund Management, Paris. HR Business Analyst, 2025 to 2026

About 20 hours a week of manual reconciliation, gone.

The problem

HR data in SAP SuccessFactors was being reviewed by hand every week, in the middle of a live HRIS transformation. Errors reached reports before anyone caught them.

What I built

I proposed and built a data-quality system: a Python FastAPI backend with a browser dashboard, running 51 checks across 500+ employee records. Polars and DuckDB pipelines ran 10 to 100 times faster than row-by-row processing.

Why it lasted

I trained HR colleagues and wrote the guides and SOPs, so the tools kept running without me. Most automation projects skip that part.

I did this as an employee. No CFM code, data or documents appear on this site.
